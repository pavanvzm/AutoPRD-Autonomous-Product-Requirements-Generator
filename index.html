The workflow

Four agents, one pipeline

Each specialist agent processes a dimension in parallel, then converges on a unified PRD.

01 · Ingestion

Collector

Ingests voice memos, Slack threads, rough notes, and competitor URLs. Transcribes and normalises everything into a unified knowledge graph.

02 · Analysis

Analyst

Extracts user pain points, market signals, and feature opportunities. Cross-references competitor data and flags gaps in the input.

03 · Synthesis

Architect

Structures the output: problem statement, goals, scope, functional requirements, success metrics, and open questions.

04 · Review

Reviewer

Audits for ambiguity, contradiction, and completeness. Generates a confidence score and suggests refinements before export.

Capabilities

What AutoPRD handles

Voice & media

Record ideas on the go. AutoPRD transcribes voice memos and extracts intent, requirements, and constraints automatically.

Competitor intelligence

Drop a competitor URL and the Analyst agent maps their positioning, feature set, and gaps — surfaced directly in your PRD.

Structured PRD export

Industry-standard sections: problem, goals, scope, requirements, metrics, risks. Export as Markdown, Notion, or PDF.

Confidence scoring

Every PRD includes a per-section confidence score. The Reviewer agent flags weak areas so you know where to add detail.

Customisable templates

Choose from standard PRD templates (CS, RICE, lean) or define your own sections. The Architect agent adapts to your format.

Revision history

Every regeneration creates a version. Compare diffs, restore earlier drafts, and track how the PRD evolved with your inputs.
